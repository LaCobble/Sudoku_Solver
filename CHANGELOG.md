## [1.0.0] - 2022-03-17

First version, as delivered for the school assignment.

### Added

- Backtracking Sudoku solver (`Sudoku.solve`) with rule checking (`estPossible`) and next-cell lookup (`caseSuivante`).
- Demo program that prints a sample grid, runs a few checks and prints the solved grid.
